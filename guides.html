Farming Guides

Practical tutorials and best practices for successful farming.

Grains • Winter • Modern

Rice Cultivation Guide

Learn land preparation, seed selection, transplanting, irrigation, fertilization, and harvesting.

  1. Prepare and level the field.
  2. Select healthy and suitable seeds.
  3. Plant seedlings at the recommended spacing.
  4. Maintain water and nutrient requirements.
  5. Harvest when the grains reach maturity.

Tip: Regular field monitoring helps identify problems early.

Vegetables • Winter • Organic

Vegetable Garden Guide

A basic guide for preparing soil and growing healthy seasonal vegetables.

  1. Choose a sunny and well-drained location.
  2. Improve soil with suitable organic matter.
  3. Choose quality seeds or seedlings.
  4. Water according to crop requirements.
  5. Remove weeds and monitor pests regularly.

Tip: Crop rotation can help maintain soil health.

Fruits • Summer • Traditional

Fruit Tree Management

Essential practices for planting, watering, pruning, and maintaining fruit trees.

  1. Select a suitable variety for the local climate.
  2. Prepare a planting hole with healthy soil.
  3. Provide proper irrigation after planting.
  4. Prune damaged and overcrowded branches.
  5. Protect developing fruits from pests.
Cash Crops • Rainy Season • Modern

Jute Farming Guide

Key steps for preparing land, sowing, maintaining, harvesting, and processing jute.

  1. Prepare fine and fertile seedbed.
  2. Sow quality seeds at suitable spacing.
  3. Thin plants and control weeds.
  4. Monitor moisture and crop health.
  5. Harvest at the appropriate growth stage.
