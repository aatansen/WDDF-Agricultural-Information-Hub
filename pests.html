Pest Control Strategies

Basic Integrated Pest Management (IPM) practices.

IPM: Monitor pests, identify them correctly, prevent problems and use suitable control methods.

Prevention

  • Use healthy seeds.
  • Keep the field clean.
  • Rotate crops when suitable.
  • Maintain proper plant spacing.

Biological Control

  • Protect beneficial insects.
  • Encourage natural predators.
  • Use approved biological products when appropriate.
  • Avoid unnecessary pesticide use.

Organic Remedies

  • Remove badly affected plant parts.
  • Maintain field hygiene.
  • Use suitable botanical products where recommended.
  • Follow safe application instructions.

Pest Monitoring

Step Action Purpose
1 Inspect plants regularly. Find pests early.
2 Identify the pest correctly. Choose the correct control method.
3 Record affected areas. Monitor changes.
4 Apply suitable treatment when needed. Reduce crop damage.
5 Check the field again. See whether the treatment worked.
Always follow product labels and local agricultural guidance when using pesticides.
