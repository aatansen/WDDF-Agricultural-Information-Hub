Pest Control Strategies

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) for healthier crops.

IPM Principle: Monitor first, identify correctly, prevent where possible, and choose the least harmful effective control method.
🛡️

1. Prevention

  • Use healthy seeds and planting materials.
  • Maintain field sanitation.
  • Rotate crops when appropriate.
  • Keep suitable spacing between plants.
🦋

2. Biological Control

  • Protect beneficial insects.
  • Encourage natural predators.
  • Use approved biological control products when appropriate.
  • Avoid unnecessary broad-spectrum pesticide use.
🌿

3. Organic Remedies

  • Remove heavily affected plant parts.
  • Use suitable botanical products where recommended.
  • Maintain good crop hygiene.
  • Follow safe application instructions.

Pest Monitoring & Early Detection

StepWhat to DoWhy It Matters
1. InspectCheck leaves, stems, roots and fruits regularly.Find problems early.
2. IdentifyDetermine the pest or disease before treatment.Prevents ineffective treatment.
3. RecordNote location, date, affected area and severity.Helps track changes over time.
4. ActUse an appropriate control method when needed.Reduces crop damage.
5. RecheckMonitor the field after treatment.Confirms whether management worked.

Important Safety Tip

Always follow the product label and local agricultural guidance when using pesticides. Wear recommended protective equipment and observe the stated waiting period.
